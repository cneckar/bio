Cris Neckar — profile

Cris Neckar is a Partner at Two Bear Capital, where he invests in cybersecurity, advanced technologies, and the intersection of technology and life sciences. He is a security researcher turned venture investor with ~17+ years in information security.

Roles & background

  • Partner, Two Bear Capital (current).
  • Original member of Google's Chrome Security Team.
  • Co-founder, Divergent Security (offensive security assessment firm).
  • Former Chief Information Security Officer, Spring Labs.
  • Led the Application Security practice at Neohapsis (now part of Cisco).
  • Adjunct professor, DePaul University cyber security graduate program.
