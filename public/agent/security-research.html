Security research — Cris Neckar

Cris Neckar was an original member of Google's Chrome Security Team and worked on browser exploitation and sandbox hardening (including the Pwnium era). He later co-founded Divergent Security and, with Lookout, performed the initial discovery and analysis of NSO Group's Pegasus spyware and the Trident iOS exploit chain (2016). While at Neohapsis he investigated the Heartland Payment Systems breach.
